Questions we hear often

Can I use your address to register my company?

Yes. Private office and virtual office members can use Building 4, Tilal Offices, Muscat Grand Mall as their registered business address, and our team can handle the Commercial Registration for you.

What do I need to sign an office lease?

Office leases run for 12 months. We ask for post-dated cheques for the term and a refundable security deposit equal to three months' rent. Landline calls are billed by usage. Bring your CR (or passport if we're setting up your company) and we'll prepare the agreement.

How quickly can I move in?

Offices are fully furnished and connected, so most members move in within a few days of signing. Meeting rooms and day passes can be booked for today.

Are meeting rooms included with my office?

Yes. Office plans include meeting rooms bookable in advance. Sign in before booking and the room fee is waived automatically.

Is there parking?

Yes — covered basement parking at Muscat Grand Mall, close to the Building 4 lifts. Members can add a reserved bay to their plan.

Can I visit before deciding?

Of course. Book a tour, or claim a free day pass and work from the 5th floor for a day.
