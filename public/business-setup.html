Setup questions

Can a foreigner own 100% of a company in Oman?

For most commercial activities, yes — Oman's Foreign Capital Investment Law allows full foreign ownership, with some activities restricted. We'll confirm what applies to your activity during the consultation.

Do I need a physical office to register?

Your company needs a registered address. A private office or virtual office at Grand Business Center gives you a Muscat Grand Mall address you can use for your Commercial Registration.

How long does company formation take?

Timelines depend on the activity and approvals needed. We'll give you a realistic timeline with your quote and keep you updated at each step.

Can you help my family relocate too?

Yes. We coordinate residential leasing and rental vehicles so you can settle in quickly.
